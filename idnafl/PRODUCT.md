# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router, TypeScript, plain CSS), statically generated. Leads go to Formspree (`NEXT_PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_ID`) tagged with the company they are for; without it the router opens a prefilled email to that company.

## Users

- Customers who know the name Iðnafl and need to find the right company for a floor, pitch, light or green.
- Partners, suppliers, municipalities and job seekers checking who Iðnafl is: people, quality system, projects.

## Product Purpose

The parent company's front door. Show the logo and "Leiðandi í lausnum" first, make the three companies and what each does obvious in seconds, and send every inquiry to the right one.

## Positioning

Iðnafl ehf. owns Gólflagnir (industrial floors and epoxy; SS Gólf merged in), Metatron (artificial-turf pitches, Musco floodlighting, maintenance) and Golfstofan (SYNLawn putting and golf greens). A 15-person team at Tónahvarf 5, Kópavogur, since May 2024.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Quality system: four 2025 policies (internal control on COSO, sustainability, social responsibility, environment), six values, UN SDGs 7, 9, 11, 12, ISO 9001, 45001 and 26000 followed (no certification claimed).
- Language: Icelandic only.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project photos from all three companies, black-and-white staff portraits, the four policy PDFs.
- No testimonials, revenue or customer counts. Do not invent them.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Iðnafl", slogan "Leiðandi í lausnum", logo of two offset outlined frames in black and red. Red #FC4236 (UI fills #dc3326). Warm paper ground, ink black. Saira and Fira Sans. Contact: Tónahvarf 5, 203 Kópavogi · 565 3600 · info@idnafl.is.

## Product Principles

1. Logo and slogan first; the three companies second; the right contact third.
2. Every card leads to the company that does the work.
3. Facts only from idnafl.is and Iðnafl's own policy documents.
