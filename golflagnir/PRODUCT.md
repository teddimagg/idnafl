# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router, TypeScript, plain CSS), statically generated. Leads go to Formspree (`NEXT_PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_ID`); without it, forms fall back to a prefilled email to golf@golflagnir.is.

## Users

- Facility and operations managers at Icelandic companies (food and fish processing, pharma, data centres, logistics, workshops, car parks) who need a floor that survives their process.
- Contractors and architects specifying floors for new builds.
- Homeowners who want an epoxy garage or bathroom floor.

## Product Purpose

Gólflagnir lays seamless industrial floors: epoxy, polyurethane, Ucrete, epoxy terrazzo, flex underlays, levelling, grinding and blastrac. SS Gólf has merged into Gólflagnir. The site must rank for "epoxy gólf", "iðnaðargólf" and their variants, and turn each visit into a quote request through a live m² price calculator.

## Positioning

The Icelandic floor specialist inside Iðnafl (sister companies Metatron and Golfstofan). Own trademarked systems (Topp 4000, Topp 1500, Maxi 700, Maxi 5000), the largest set of grinding machines in the country, and clients such as atNorth, Alvotech, Marel, Arion banki, Landspítali and Eimskip.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 10 floor systems, 13 uses, price ranges per m² in `lib/pricing.ts` (**estimates the client must confirm**).
- Data sheets are being rewritten by the client; the old PDFs must not be migrated.
- Language: Icelandic only.
