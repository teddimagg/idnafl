## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router, TypeScript, plain CSS), statically generated. Leads go to Formspree (`NEXT_PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_ID`); without it the inquiry falls back to a prefilled email to info@metatron.is.

## Users

- Municipalities and sports clubs planning a new artificial-turf pitch, a turf replacement, floodlighting or a maintenance contract.
- Facility managers of sports halls, schools and playgrounds.
- Homeowners and businesses who want artificial grass in a garden or on a balcony.

## Product Purpose

Metatron lays artificial-turf football pitches from A to Ö, installs Musco floodlighting and German irrigation systems, maintains pitches all year, and sells and lays SYNLawn garden turf. The site must rank for "gervigras", "gervigrasvellir", "flóðlýsing", "garðagras" and related searches, and turn every visit into an inquiry.

## Positioning

Iceland's leading artificial-turf and sports-lighting contractor, part of Iðnafl (sister companies Gólflagnir and Golfstofan). 57 full-size pitches, over 30 large lighting systems, FIFA Quality and FIFA Quality PRO pitches.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Garden turf prices per m² come from the metatron.is shop; minimum order 10 m²; 15-year warranty.
- No published prices for pitches, lighting or maintenance plans; those are quoted.
- Golf work belongs to Golfstofan and is linked there, not duplicated.
- Language: Icelandic only.
