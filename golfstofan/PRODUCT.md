## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router, TypeScript, plain CSS). Requested by the user. Leads go to Formspree (`NEXT_PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_ID`); without it the forms fall back to a prefilled email to info@idnafl.is.

## Users

- Homeowners in the Reykjavík area who want a garden, balcony or home putting green without mowing, mud or moss. Most are not golfers, so the page has to explain what this is before anything else.
- Golf clubs and practice facilities that want greens, tees or practice areas (Golfklúbburinn Oddur is the reference).

## Product Purpose

Golfstofan sells and installs SYNLawn artificial grass in Iceland. The website is a lead generator: the visitor should understand the offer in seconds, see a price for their own m², and leave a name and phone number.

## Positioning

The official Icelandic partner of SYNLawn, the turf used in TGL (Tiger Woods and Rory McIlroy's league with the PGA Tour). Installed the first TGL system in Europe, at Golfklúbburinn Oddur. Decades of turf installation experience in Iceland. Part of Iðnafl ehf., working closely with Metatron (installation) and Musco (lighting).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Products: PrecisionPutt (10 mm, 10–12 STIMP), TeeStrike+ (29 mm tee turf), SYNNatural 50L (50 mm), SYNAugustine 347 (45 mm), SYNFringe (25 mm), shock pad.
- Price estimator uses reference rates in `lib/pricing.ts`. **These are researched estimates, not Golfstofan's price list, and the client must confirm them before launch.**
- Language: Icelandic only.
